# Content Review

Last reviewed: 2026-05-03

This is a lightweight deployment-safety note, not legal advice. The project is
configured for a US-hosted GitHub Pages deployment, so the working rule for
revival is: only ship texts that are safe to redistribute in the United States.

## Removed From Deployable Content

- `1984` by George Orwell was removed. It was published in 1949 and is not
  expected to enter the US public domain until 2045 if its copyright was
  properly renewed.
- `Animal Farm` by George Orwell was removed. It was published in 1945 and is
  generally expected to remain protected in the US until 2041.
- The movie/script section was removed because the revived project is books-only
  and the existing screenplay was not appropriate for a public-domain book
  library.

## Current Review Notes

- As of 2026 in the United States, works published in 1930 have entered the
  public domain; older publication dates are a useful initial screen, but each
  text and edition still needs its own provenance check.
- Keep source URLs, edition notes, and public-domain rationale with any future
  additions.
